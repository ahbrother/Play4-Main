DRIFT – Where Parties Move When the Economy Shifts

Why do political landscapes change so rapidly? This project visualizes how economic factors influence political shifts. It also highlights how political parties change their ideological positions over time.

Through our installation, you can explore both temporal and geographical changes in politics and the economy. Explore interactively how economic conditions relate to voting behavior while recognizing that they are only one part of a complex mix of social, cultural, and other influences.

If a country is not avaiable, it is not included in the dataset.
